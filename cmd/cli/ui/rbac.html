Who may build and sign

A supply chain's ServiceAccount is given a signing identity only while it passes three checks. Each is a question put to the cluster's own access control, and the answer is recorded here.

Who may write admission policy

An admission policy is rendered on behalf of a ServiceAccount, which has to pass three checks of its own before anything is rendered.
